How it works

  • Your phone makes the door: a key pair. The sticker carries the public half; the private half never leaves the phone that made it.
  • A visitor's phone makes a fresh line and seals its key to your door. The door holds that sealed knock for a day, taps your phone, and can open nothing itself.
  • You answer from /door; the line opens on both phones. Unlimited knocks, unlimited lines.
  • The door is yours as long as it's paid. Lapse, and the sticker is paper until you renew; nothing was read either way.
