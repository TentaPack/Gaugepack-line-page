Terms. Plain words.

A private line is a service of TentaPack LLC, Chicago, Illinois, offered under the Gaugepack name. Using it, buying a line, a door, or an organisation's pack means you accept these terms. They are written to be read.

What you get

  • A line is a room that opens only to the stickers that carry its key. A pair or a pack is one line for one year from the day it is made. You may renew it with the renewal code the room shows.
  • A door is one sticker that opens a fresh line with the phone that made the door. It is a subscription, monthly or yearly, unlimited knocks, and stays open while it is paid.
  • An organisation's pack is an account that makes, prints, reissues, closes and reopens lines for a business, billed monthly at $2 a line with a minimum of 25 lines, invoiced by Gaugepack. The account holds ids, labels and dates, never a key.
  • Everything is sealed on your phone. We hold noise or nothing; see privacy.

Paying
