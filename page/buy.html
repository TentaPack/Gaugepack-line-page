Gaugepack · A private line

A line, one year. Nobody can read it. Not even us.

Pay once for a year. After paying, your phone makes the line itself: the key is made on your phone, printed as stickers, and never sent to us. The payment says "a line, one year", and nothing about which line. We never connect a card to a room.

Renew a line you already have

Open the line on your phone and tap "Renew" under the fingerprint words: it shows a six-letter code, good for ten minutes. Type it here and buy a year. The code points at your line only while it lives; afterwards nobody, including us, can tell which line was renewed.

For a business: a door

One sticker that opens a fresh private line with whoever printed it, unlimited. $19 a month or $190 a year. The door, explained.

What the payment knows

  • Stripe takes the card and asks for an email for the receipt; that is Stripe's record of a purchase called "a private line, one year". It never learns which line, because the line does not exist until after you pay.
  • We keep a note that a payment was made and claimed, as a hash of the checkout, so it cannot be claimed twice. Not the room, not the key, not you.
  • A renewal pairs the checkout with its room for as long as the checkout is open, half an hour at most, then forgets. Everything else about a line is in the promise: what we hold.
